Pure-Go, MRI-faithful Ruby rspec (no cgo). It is a pure-Go (no cgo) reimplementation of Ruby's rspec, mirroring its observable behaviour without any Ruby runtime. It is a standalone, reusable module — the backend bound into go-embedded-ruby by rbgo, the same pattern as go-ruby-yaml — differential-tested against reference Ruby, 100% coverage, CI green across 6 arches and 3 OSes.
